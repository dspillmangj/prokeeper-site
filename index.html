Two Complete Professional Sports Engines

Engineered specifically for the unique rules and fast tempo of collegiate and high school athletics.

Basketball Stat Engine

Track all 19 standardized actions including 2pt, 3pt, free throws, offensive/defensive rebounds, assists, steals, blocks, swats, and turnover types (pass, fumble, violation). Includes automated playing time calculation based on on-court substitutions.

Volleyball Rally & Rotation Engine

Rally scoring sets 1 through 5 with win-by-2 verification. Automatic 6-position court rotation shifts on side-outs. Complete hitting percentage calculations $((\text{Kills} - \text{Errors}) / \text{Attempts})$, aces, solo blocks, and libero substitution tracking.

Authentic ProKeeper Digital Scorebook

Digital reproduction of the official scorebook layout. Features the running score point slash matrix, individual personal fouls 1-5 with DQ warnings, team fouls per period, and instantaneous 1-click official PDF export.
